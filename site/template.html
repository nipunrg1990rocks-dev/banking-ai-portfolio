How every project is built

Six rules I applied to all five

Decisions

Rules decide, models assist

Verdicts and routing come from explainable rules that give the same answer every time. The model drafts, explains and challenges.

Accountability

A person approves regulated actions

Sanctions, KYC, credit and filings always reach a named human, with maker-checker and segregation of duties.

Grounding

Every number traces to a source

An AI answer that cites a section, transaction or figure that doesn't exist is rejected before anyone sees it.

Audit

Every step is on the record

Agent steps and approvals go into a SHA-256 hash chain that shows any later edit, like the one at the top of this page.

Cost and privacy

Runs free, works offline

Each app runs with no paid AI by default. Live models are free Databricks foundation models, and personal data is redacted first.

Evidence

Measured, not claimed

Synthetic data ships with a hidden answer key of planted issues, and the tests must find them exactly.

How these were built

These are independent projects built with AI-assisted development tools. The split of work was clear:

  • I decidedThe use cases, the control rules, what AI may and may not do, and the acceptance criteria.
  • I designedThe test scenarios and answer keys: planted defects, decoys, false positives and prompt injections.
  • AI tools wroteThe code, under my direction, to the designs and controls above.
  • I verifiedThe test results, the app behaviour and the Databricks runs, and I record anything unproven as unproven.
