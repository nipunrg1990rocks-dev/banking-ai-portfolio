Ledgerline Compliance Copilot

An AI policy assistant for bank staff. It answers KYC, AML, sanctions and risk questions only from approved policy text, cites the exact section, shows how confident it is, and sends anything that touches a regulated decision to a human reviewer.

Try the live demo ↗ View the code on GitHub ↗

Independent project built by Nipun Gupta with AI-assisted development tools. All policies and data are synthetic and describe a fictional bank. It is not affiliated with any employer.

The banking problem

Wrong policy answers become regulatory breaches

Operations and KYC teams search long policy manuals every day. An answer from memory can mean a missed review date, a released sanctions hit, or a customer being tipped off about a SAR. Generic chatbots can't cite policy, keep no audit trail, and may invent rules. This copilot is built around the controls a bank's second line and model-risk team would expect.

How it works

One question, five controlled steps

Guardrails

Personal data is removed. Prompt-injection attempts are blocked.

Retrieve

Hybrid search (keyword + semantic) finds the five most relevant policy sections.

Answer

Quotes policy sentences with citations. An optional LLM mode must cite real sources or it is rejected.

Check & route

Confidence score and risk rules decide: release, human review, or refuse.

Audit

Every step goes into a hash-chained log that shows any later tampering.

Banking controls built in

Governance by design

  • Maker-checker review: the person who asks can't approve their own answer
  • Tipping-off detection puts the SAR confidentiality rule first
  • Refuses to answer when the policies don't cover the question
  • Tamper-evident audit trail, verifiable and exportable
  • Runs free by default with no external AI calls, so no data leaves the app
