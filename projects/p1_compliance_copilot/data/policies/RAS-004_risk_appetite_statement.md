# Enterprise Risk Appetite Statement

This is a synthetic risk appetite statement for the fictional Ledgerline Bank. All figures are illustrative.

## 1. Purpose

The Risk Appetite Statement defines the amount and type of risk Ledgerline Bank is willing to accept in pursuit of its strategy. It is approved annually by the Board Risk Committee and cascaded into business-line limits.

## 2. Appetite Levels

The bank uses four appetite levels: Averse (avoid the risk wherever possible), Minimal (accept only very low levels), Cautious (accept moderate risk with strong controls) and Open (accept higher risk for higher return). The bank has an Averse appetite for financial crime, sanctions breaches and regulatory non-compliance. It has a Cautious appetite for credit risk and a Minimal appetite for operational risk losses.

## 3. Capital and Liquidity Metrics

The Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio must remain at or above 11.5 percent (limit), with an early-warning trigger at 12.5 percent. The Liquidity Coverage Ratio must remain at or above 110 percent, with an early-warning trigger at 120 percent.

## 4. Credit Risk Metrics

Non-performing loans must not exceed 2.5 percent of the gross loan book, with an early-warning trigger at 2.0 percent. Exposure to any single counterparty group must not exceed 10 percent of Tier 1 capital.

## 5. Operational and Compliance Risk Metrics

Annual operational risk losses must not exceed 15 million US dollars, with an early-warning trigger at 10 million US dollars. The bank tolerates zero confirmed sanctions breaches. Overdue High risk KYC periodic reviews must not exceed 2 percent of the High risk population. Late SAR filings are tolerated at zero.

## 6. Breach and Escalation

When an early-warning trigger is reached, the metric owner must notify the Chief Risk Officer within 5 business days and present a remediation plan. When a limit is breached, the metric owner must notify the Chief Risk Officer within 2 business days, and the CRO must inform the Board Risk Committee at its next meeting or within 30 days, whichever is sooner. Breaches of zero-tolerance metrics, such as a confirmed sanctions breach, must be reported to the Board Risk Committee chair within 24 hours.

## 7. Monitoring and Reporting

Risk appetite metrics are reported monthly to the Executive Risk Committee and quarterly to the Board Risk Committee, using a red, amber and green status. Red means a limit breach, amber means an early-warning trigger has been reached, and green means within appetite.
