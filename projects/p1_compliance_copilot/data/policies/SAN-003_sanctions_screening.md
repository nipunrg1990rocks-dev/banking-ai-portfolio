# Sanctions Screening and Escalation Standard

This is a synthetic standard written for the fictional Ledgerline Bank. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This standard sets the controls Ledgerline Bank uses to avoid dealing with sanctioned individuals, entities, vessels and jurisdictions. It applies to customer screening, payment screening and trade finance transactions in every business line.

## 2. Lists Screened

The bank screens against the consolidated sanctions lists adopted by the Sanctions Compliance function, including government designation lists and internal watch lists. List updates must be loaded into the screening system within 24 hours of publication. A failed or delayed list update is a Severity 2 incident under the Operational Risk and Incident Management Policy (ORM-005).

## 3. Customer Screening

All new customers, beneficial owners and controlling persons must be screened before account opening. The full existing customer base must be re-screened every time the lists are updated (delta screening) and fully re-screened at least once per month.

## 4. Payment Screening

All cross-border payments and all domestic wires must be screened in real time before release. Payments that generate an alert are held automatically and must not be released until the alert is dispositioned.

## 5. Match Thresholds

The fuzzy matching threshold for names is set at 85 percent similarity. Any change to the threshold requires a documented impact assessment, testing evidence and approval from the Head of Sanctions Compliance and Model Risk Management.

## 6. Alert Disposition

Level 1 analysts must review payment screening alerts within 4 business hours and customer screening alerts within 2 business days. A false positive may be closed by a Level 1 analyst with a documented rationale identifying the differentiating data (for example date of birth, nationality or address). An alert may not be closed as a false positive based on name alone.

## 7. Potential True Match Escalation

A potential true match must be escalated to the Sanctions Compliance Officer within 1 hour of identification. The payment or account must remain blocked or frozen. No potential true match may be released without second-level approval from the Sanctions Compliance Officer. Confirmed true matches must be blocked or rejected in line with applicable sanctions law and reported to the relevant authority within the legally required timeframe, which Sanctions Compliance tracks for each regime.

## 8. Prohibited Activity

Employees must not advise customers on how to avoid sanctions controls, alter or remove information in payment messages (wire stripping), or split transactions to avoid screening. Any such request from a customer must be escalated to Sanctions Compliance immediately.

## 9. Testing and Assurance

The screening system must undergo annual effectiveness testing, including name-variation testing and list completeness checks. Results are reported to the Financial Crime Risk Committee.
