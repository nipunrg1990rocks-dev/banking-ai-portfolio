# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

This is a synthetic policy for the fictional Ledgerline Bank. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs how Ledgerline Bank selects, contracts, monitors and exits third parties, including technology vendors, cloud and AI service providers, outsourcing providers and fintech partners.

## 2. Criticality Assessment

Each third party is assessed as Critical, High, Moderate or Low based on the impact of its failure on customers, operations and regulatory obligations. Any provider that processes Restricted data or supports a critical business service is at least High.

## 3. Due Diligence

Before contract signature, Critical and High third parties must complete due diligence covering financial health, information security, business continuity, data privacy, sanctions screening of the vendor and its owners, and subcontractor (fourth-party) arrangements. AI service providers must additionally disclose how customer data is used for model training and where data is processed.

## 4. Contract Requirements

Contracts with Critical and High third parties must include audit rights, data protection terms, incident notification within 24 hours, business continuity commitments, and exit and transition assistance.

## 5. Ongoing Monitoring

Critical third parties must be reassessed annually, High every two years, and Moderate every three years. Performance against service levels must be reviewed at least quarterly for Critical providers.

## 6. Exit Planning

Every Critical third party must have a documented and tested exit plan describing how the service would be transferred or brought in-house within a defined timeframe without unacceptable disruption to customers.
