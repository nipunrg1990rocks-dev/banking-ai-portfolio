# Records Retention and Data Privacy Standard

This is a synthetic standard for the fictional Ledgerline Bank. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This standard sets minimum retention periods for business records and the privacy controls applied to personal data held by Ledgerline Bank.

## 2. Retention Periods

Customer identification and due diligence records: 5 years after the relationship ends. Transaction records: 5 years from the date of the transaction. Suspicious Activity Reports and supporting documentation: 5 years from filing. Audit logs of AI and model systems: at least 5 years. Complaints records: 7 years. Board and committee minutes: permanently.

## 3. Legal Hold

When litigation, a regulatory investigation or an audit is anticipated, Legal may place records on legal hold. Records on legal hold must not be deleted even if their retention period has expired, until Legal releases the hold.

## 4. Data Classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data, account numbers and SAR information are Restricted. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and in transit, and access must be granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis with quarterly access recertification.

## 5. Personal Data Minimisation

Employees must collect and process only the personal data needed for a defined purpose. Personal data must not be entered into unapproved tools, including public generative AI services. Test and demonstration environments must use synthetic or properly anonymised data.

## 6. Secure Disposal

Records that have reached the end of their retention period and are not on legal hold must be securely destroyed within 90 days. Destruction must be logged with the record category, date and approver.

## 7. Data Breach Response

A suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Privacy Office within 24 hours of discovery. The Privacy Office decides on regulator and customer notification within the legally required timeframe.
