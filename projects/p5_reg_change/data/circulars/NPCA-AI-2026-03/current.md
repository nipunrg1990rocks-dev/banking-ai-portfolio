# Use of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI by Regulated Firms

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only. It is a new circular with no previous version.

## 1. Inventory

Firms must register every AI system in an inventory before use, recording its owner, purpose and risk tier.

## 2. Accountability

Firms must designate a senior manager who is personally accountable for the firm's use of AI and who reports on AI risk to the board.

## 3. Human Oversight

AI systems must not make final decisions on credit, onboarding or financial crime matters without review by a qualified human.

## 4. Generative AI

Generative AI applications must show citations to their source documents and must log prompts and responses. Firms must retain generative AI audit logs for at least seven years.

## 5. Fairness Testing

Firms must test customer-facing AI systems for bias and unfair outcomes before deployment and at least every six months thereafter.

## 6. Incident Notification

Firms must notify the Authority of a material AI incident within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 7. Transparency

Firms must tell customers when they are interacting with an AI system rather than a person.
