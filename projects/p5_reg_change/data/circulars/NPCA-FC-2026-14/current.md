# Transaction Monitoring and Suspicious Activity Reporting (Amended)

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Application

These rules apply to all firms authorised to accept deposits. Firms must operate transaction monitoring that covers all accounts and payment channels, including wire transfers, cards, cash, digital wallets, instant payments and crypto-asset transfers.

## 2. Monitoring Scenarios

Firms must tune transaction monitoring scenario thresholds at least every six months and must document below-the-line testing for each tuning exercise.

## 3. Alert Handling

Firms must triage every alert within 5 business days of generation. Firms must complete the investigation of an alert within 30 calendar days of alert generation.

## 4. Suspicious Activity Reports

Firms must file a suspicious activity report no later than 15 calendar days after the date of initial determination of suspicion. Where no suspect is identified, filing must not exceed 45 calendar days after initial detection. Firms must review continuing activity at least every 90 days after a report is filed.

## 5. Authorised Push Payment Fraud

Firms must operate monitoring scenarios that detect money mule accounts receiving the proceeds of authorised push payment fraud. Firms must report authorised push payment fraud typologies and mule account volumes to the board at least quarterly.

## 6. Model Validation

Firms must treat transaction monitoring systems as models and must have them independently validated at least annually.

## 7. Record Keeping

Firms must retain suspicious activity reports and supporting records for at least five years from the date of filing.
