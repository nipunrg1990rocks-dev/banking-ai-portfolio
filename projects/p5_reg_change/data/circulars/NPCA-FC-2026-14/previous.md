# Transaction Monitoring and Suspicious Activity Reporting

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Application

These rules apply to all firms authorised to accept deposits. Firms must operate transaction monitoring that covers all accounts and payment channels, including wire transfers, cards, cash and digital wallets.

## 2. Monitoring Scenarios

Firms must tune transaction monitoring scenario thresholds at least annually.

## 3. Alert Handling

Firms must triage every alert within 10 business days of generation. Firms must complete the investigation of an alert within 30 calendar days of alert generation.

## 4. Suspicious Activity Reports

Firms must file a suspicious activity report no later than 30 calendar days after the date of initial determination of suspicion. Where no suspect is identified, filing must not exceed 60 calendar days after initial detection. Firms must review continuing activity at least every 90 days after a report is filed. Firms with fewer than 50 employees may file reports on paper.

## 5. Record Keeping

Firms must retain suspicious activity reports and supporting records for at least five years from the date of filing.
