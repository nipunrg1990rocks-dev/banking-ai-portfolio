# Sanctions Screening (Amended)

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. List Updates

Firms must load sanctions list updates into their screening systems within 4 hours of publication.

## 2. Customer Screening

Firms must re-screen their full customer base at least monthly.

## 3. Escalation

Firms must escalate a potential true match to a sanctions officer within 1 hour of identification.

## 4. Matching Thresholds

Firms must document and test their fuzzy matching thresholds at least annually.

## 5. Reporting Frozen Assets

Firms must report frozen assets to the Authority within 10 business days.

## 6. Payment Message Parties

Firms must screen the names of all parties to a payment message, including intermediary banks.
