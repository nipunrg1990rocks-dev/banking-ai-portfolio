# Sanctions Screening

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. List Updates

Firms must load sanctions list updates into their screening systems within 48 hours of publication.

## 2. Customer Screening

Firms must re-screen their full customer base at least every three months.

## 3. Escalation

Firms must escalate a potential true match to a sanctions officer within 1 hour of identification.

## 4. Payment Screening

Firms may screen domestic low-value payments in batch at the end of each day.
