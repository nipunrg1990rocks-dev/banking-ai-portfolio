# Operational Resilience and Incident Reporting

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Incident Notification

Firms must notify the Authority of a major operational incident within 24 hours of identification. Firms must submit a paper copy of each incident report by post.

## 2. Outsourcing

Contracts with material outsourcing providers must require incident notification within 72 hours. Firms must have exit plans for material outsourcing arrangements.

## 3. Loss Data

Firms must record operational losses of 10,000 US dollars or more.

## 4. Self-Assessment

Firms must complete a risk and control self-assessment at least annually.
