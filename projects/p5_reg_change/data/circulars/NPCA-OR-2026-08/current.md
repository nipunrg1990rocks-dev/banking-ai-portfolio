# Operational Resilience and Incident Reporting (Amended)

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Incident Notification

Firms must notify the Authority of a major operational incident within 4 hours of identification.

## 2. Important Business Services

Firms must set an impact tolerance for each important business service and test their ability to remain within it at least annually.

## 3. Outsourcing

Contracts with material outsourcing providers must require incident notification within 24 hours. Firms must have exit plans for material outsourcing arrangements and test them at least every two years.

## 4. Loss Data

Firms must record operational losses of 5,000 US dollars or more.

## 5. Self-Assessment

Firms must complete a risk and control self-assessment at least annually.
