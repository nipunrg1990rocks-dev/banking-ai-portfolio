# Customer Due Diligence and Beneficial Ownership

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Beneficial Ownership

Firms must identify and verify every individual who owns or controls 25 percent or more of a legal entity customer. Firms must document ownership structures with more than three layers and review them before account opening.

## 2. Politically Exposed Persons

Firms must apply enhanced due diligence to foreign politically exposed persons.

## 3. Ongoing Review

Firms must refresh due diligence for high risk customers at least every 12 months, for medium risk customers at least every 36 months and for low risk customers at least every 60 months. A periodic review must not remain overdue for more than 90 days.

## 4. Event-Driven Review

Firms must complete an event-driven review within 30 days of a trigger event such as a change in ownership or a confirmed sanctions match.

## 5. Record Keeping

Firms must retain customer due diligence records for at least five years after the business relationship ends.
