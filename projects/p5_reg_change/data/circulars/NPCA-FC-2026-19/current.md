# Customer Due Diligence and Beneficial Ownership (Revised)

This is a synthetic regulatory circular issued by a fictional regulator. It is used for demonstration only.

## 1. Beneficial Ownership

Firms must identify and verify every individual who owns or controls 10 percent or more of a legal entity customer. Firms must document ownership structures with more than three layers and review them before account opening.

## 2. Politically Exposed Persons

Firms must apply enhanced due diligence to foreign and domestic politically exposed persons, and to their family members and close associates. Firms must continue to treat a customer as a politically exposed person for at least 12 months after the person leaves public office.

## 3. Ongoing Review

Firms must refresh due diligence for high risk customers at least every 12 months, for medium risk customers at least every 24 months and for low risk customers at least every 36 months. A periodic review must not remain overdue for more than 30 days.

## 4. Event-Driven Review

Firms must complete an event-driven review within 10 business days of a trigger event such as a change in ownership or a confirmed sanctions match.

## 5. Remote Onboarding

Firms that onboard customers remotely must verify identity through a certified digital identity provider with liveness detection.

## 6. Record Keeping

Firms must retain customer due diligence records for at least five years after the business relationship ends.
